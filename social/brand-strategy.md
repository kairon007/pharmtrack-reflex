# Brand Strategy & Persona

## Core Identity

**Tagline:** "Builder over debator"

**Positioning:** A product engineer who ships across boundaries while others debate frameworks.

## Persona: The Pragmatic Builder

### Voice Characteristics
- **Confident but grounded** - Shares results, not theories
- **Direct** - Cuts through tech tribalism
- **Action-oriented** - "Shipping > debating" is the anthem
- **Experiential** - Writes from real builds, not blog posts
- **Slightly contrarian** - Challenges the "wait for perfect tool" mindset

### Core Beliefs (Your Content Pillars)

| Pillar | Theme | Example Hook |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| **Ship It** | Real apps in real stores | "No demo-level builds" |
| **Cross-Boundary Thinking** | Stack-agnostic engineering | Swift on Android, Flutter, KMP - whatever works |
| **Product Over Tool** | User problems > technology debates | "Be a product engineer, not a framework devotee" |
| **Adapt or Stagnate** | Learning in public | "Ecosystems compete, builders adapt" |

### Content Angles That Resonate

**Proven Winners (from your first post):**
1. "I didn't wait" - No-more-excuses framing
2. Dual-store proof - iOS + Android links as social proof
3. Lessons learned list - Shareable, quotable insights
4. Question to audience - Engagement driver

**Future Post Ideas:**
- Build timeline / speed to market
- Specific technical challenges solved (with code when useful)
- What you'd do differently
- Tool comparison (fair, experience-based)
- Hiring/team philosophy as a builder
- Failures and pivots (authentic, human)

## Visual/Format Preferences

- **Screenshots perform** - App screenshots, builds, real UI
- **Short paragraphs** - LinkedIn favors scannable
- **Emoji minimal** - Your post had none, keep that edge
- **Line breaks** - Breathe between thought blocks
- **Hashtags** - Selective: #BuildInPublic, #MobileDev, #ProductEngineer

## Posting Cadence & Topics

### Weekly Rhythm (suggested)
- **1x Substantive post** - Case study, lesson, or opinion
- **1x Quick win/insight** - Single thought, screenshot, or question

### Topic Rotation
1. **Week 1:** PharmTrack story deep-dive (technical)
2. **Week 2:** Opinion piece (framework debates, "waiting" culture)
3. **Week 3:** Learning/mistake post (vulnerability + value)
4. **Week 4:** Community question or engagement post

## Engagement Triggers

- Ask questions at end of posts
- Reply to comments with substance
- Engage with other builders, not just tech Twitter
- Share metrics transparently when appropriate

## Tone Dos & Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Show the apps | Just talk about tools |
| Be opinionated | Be inflammatory |
| Share concrete numbers | Speculate without proof |
| Acknowledge tradeoffs | Dismissing other approaches entirely |
| Invite dialogue | Lecture |
