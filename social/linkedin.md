Swift on Android is exciting.

But I didn’t wait for it.

I challenged myself to ship a real product across both ecosystems — not as a Swift developer, not as an iOS specialist — but as someone who believes good products shouldn’t be limited by stack boundaries.

So I built PharmTrack and got it live on both stores:

• iOS → https://lnkd.in/e3GJwmuj
• Android → https://lnkd.in/euYTsw-H

No shortcuts. No “demo-level” builds.

A real app, reviewed, approved, and in users’ hands.

What this experiment taught me:

• The hardest part isn’t syntax — it’s platform thinking
• Each ecosystem has its own opinionated UX and constraints
• Shipping > debating frameworks
• You don’t need to be a “Swift dev” or “Kotlin dev” — you need to be a product engineer

While the industry debates Swift vs Kotlin vs KMP,
the real leverage is in being able to move across them when needed.

Tools will evolve.

Ecosystems will compete.

But builders who can adapt will always have the edge.

Curious — are you waiting for the perfect cross-platform solution,
or are you already shipping across boundaries?
