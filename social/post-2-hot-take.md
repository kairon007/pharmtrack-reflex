Your app isn't built because you're still waiting for the perfect framework.

While you were comparing Kotlin vs Swift vs Flutter vs KMP - I finished PharmTrack.

It's live on iOS and Android. Real users. Real reviews. Real downloads.

Here's the uncomfortable truth:

The "perfect" cross-platform tool doesn't exist. And it never will.

Each ecosystem evolves. Each has its own opinions. The moment you think you've found "the answer," something new launches.

So what's the actual move?

**Ship with what works today.**

Your constraint is rarely the framework. It's the delay.

I used Skip to bridge Swift → Android. Was it perfect? No. Did it work? Yes. Is the app in users' hands? Absolutely.

The people building real products aren't waiting for permission or perfection.

They're building, shipping, and iterating.

The "cross-platform doesn't work" crowd has been wrong before. They're wrong now.

**What are you still waiting for?**
