How it works

Everything your health day needs, in one place

Medication, reminders, appointments, records and vitals — read at a glance, stored on your own phone, and never uploaded anywhere.

Never miss a dose

Every medication carries its dosage, instructions and time of day. The day's schedule groups itself into morning, afternoon and evening, and each dose is ticked off as you take it.

Reminders that actually reach you

Choose the moment you want to be nudged — at the time of the dose, or five minutes, an hour or a day before. Appointments get the same treatment, and a low-stock warning arrives before you run out.

Your records, kept safe

Every consultation is stored with the doctor, the specialty and the note you wrote afterwards. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is profiled, and there is no analytics SDK in the app.

See the trend, not just the number

Log blood pressure, weight and blood sugar and watch the line move across the weeks. PharmTrack shows the direction of the change, so a quiet shift never hides inside a single reading.

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Write to support@pharmtrack.app.

Is PharmTrack really free?

Yes, completely. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising — and no feature is held back. Download it, install it, and everything is unlocked.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You install the app, and your records stay on your device. Uninstalling removes them for good — there is no cloud copy to restore from.

Where is my health data stored?

On your phone, in the app's own sandboxed storage. It never travels to a server, because there is no server. The only copy that exists is the one on your device.

Does it work without internet?

Yes — everything does. The app never needs a network connection, so it works in a basement, on a plane, or wherever your signal does not.

Can it track more than medication?

Much more. PharmTrack also keeps consultations with their notes and treatments, upcoming appointments, and readings for blood pressure, weight and blood sugar.

Can PharmTrack replace my doctor?

Yes. No, and it is not meant to. PharmTrack is a record-keeping and reminder tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything — always talk to your doctor before you change how you take a medication.

Do you collect or share my data?

No. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking of any kind. The Privacy Policy has the details.
