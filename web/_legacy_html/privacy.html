Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2024

1. Introduction

PharmTrack ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our application and on our website.

2. Data We Collect

PharmTrack is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal data:

  • Health Data You Enter: Medications, consultations, appointments, vital signs - stored entirely on your device
  • Device Information: Only basic info needed for app functionality (OS version, app version)
  • Analytics: None. We do not track your usage or behavior
  • Identifiers: We do not collect any personally identifiable information

3. How We Use Your Data

All health data you enter into PharmTrack is stored exclusively on your device. We:

  • Do NOT upload your data to any server
  • Do NOT share your data with third parties
  • Do NOT sell your data
  • Do NOT use your data for advertising
  • Do NOT analyze your health information

4. Data Storage

Your health information is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve it.

  • iOS: Data stored in app's encrypted sandbox
  • Android: Data stored in app's encrypted sandbox
  • Backup: Only backed up if you enable device backup

5. Data Security

We implement strong security measures:

  • Encrypted local storage on your device
  • No network transmission of health data
  • Regular security updates
  • No accounts or logins required

6. Third-Party Services

PharmTrack does NOT use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not integrate with:

  • Google Analytics or similar
  • Ad networks
  • Health data platforms
  • Cloud storage services

7. Children's Privacy

PharmTrack does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Parents may use the app to manage their child's medications under parental supervision.

8. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual entries anytime from within the app
  • Clear all data by uninstalling the app
  • No cloud copies mean no data recovery needed

9. Website Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking. We may use technical cookies only for essential functionality.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or website.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@pharmtrack.app
  • Website: your-domain.com

12. Legal Compliance

PharmTrack complies with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • HIPAA principles for health data security
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • App Store and Play Store privacy guidelines

Summary

PharmTrack is fundamentally different from other health apps:

  • ✅ Your data stays on your device
  • ✅ We never access your health information
  • ✅ No accounts or logins
  • ✅ No tracking or analytics
  • ✅ No ads
  • ✅ Complete privacy and control
