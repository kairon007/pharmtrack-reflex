Support

We read every message.

Something not behaving the way it should, or a question about how a feature works? Write to us and we will get back to you.

Common questions

  • My data looks wrong after an update. Uninstalling removes everything; reinstalling restores nothing, because there is no cloud copy. If you had a device backup before the update, you can restore from it.
  • Notifications are not arriving. Check the app's notification settings, then your device settings for PharmTrack, and make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them.
  • A medication is missing from the schedule. Deactivated medications are hidden from today's list — check the Inventory tab.
  • I want to start over. Clear all data from within the app's settings, or uninstall it. Both are permanent.

Report a bug

Please include your device model, the operating system version, the app version and what you expected to happen instead. That is usually enough to reproduce it.
