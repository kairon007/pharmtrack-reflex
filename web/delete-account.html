Delete your data

There is no account to delete — and that is on purpose.

PharmTrack has no sign-up, no login and no server. There is no account for us to close, because there is no copy of your health data anywhere but your own device.

Delete everything

Open the app's settings and choose the option that clears all data. That removes every medication, consultation, appointment and reading from the device, and cancels the reminders that go with them.

  • Uninstalling works too. Removing the app deletes the local file along with it.
  • Deleting one item is enough. Every record can be removed on its own, from the list it belongs to.

What we can and cannot do

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you want a copy of what you entered before you clear it, take a screenshot or a device backup first — once it is gone, it is gone.
